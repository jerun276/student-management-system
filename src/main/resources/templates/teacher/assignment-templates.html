Template Management Guide

✅ What you can do:

  • Edit Templates: Change title, description, due date for entire classroom
  • Delete Templates: Remove assignment from all students in classroom
  • Create New: Add new assignments for specific classrooms
  • Bulk Management: Affect all students at once

⚠️ Important Notes:

  • Classroom-Wide: Changes affect ALL students in the classroom
  • No Individual Edits: Cannot edit assignments for individual students
  • Consistency: Ensures all students have identical assignments
  • Grading Separate: Individual grading is done from assignment details
